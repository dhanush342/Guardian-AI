How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Guardian AI maintains strict data protection standards to safeguard personal information, usage metrics, and content processes.

Information Collection

  • Personal & Billing Data: Includes user name, email address, company profile, and payment details.
  • System & Usage Data: Captures IP addresses, device metadata, system activity logs, and login timestamps.
  • Content Data: Processes submitted text, images, and AI-generated output for safety inspection.

Data Utilization

Collected data is processed strictly to maintain platform services, handle subscription billing, optimize AI safety models, detect fraud or unauthorized access, and issue system alerts.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

Personal data is never sold. Third-party disclosure is restricted exclusively to essential infrastructure providers (e.g., AWS cloud hosting), payment gateways (Stripe, Razorpay), and legal authorities when strictly mandated by applicable laws.

Data Retention & Controls

Default data retention schedules are structured by account tier at 7, 30, or 90 days, with custom retention durations available. Users retain the right to request full data export or permanent deletion at any time.

Regulatory Compliance

Designed in full alignment with international privacy standards, including GDPR for European users, the India IT Rules, and CCPA framework controls.
