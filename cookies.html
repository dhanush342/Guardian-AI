Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can retain information across visits. This policy describes browser storage used by the current Guardian AI website.

Essential Authentication Storage

Guardian AI uses Supabase Auth to manage sign-in sessions. The Supabase browser client stores session information, including access and refresh tokens, in local storage so you can stay signed in when navigating between pages or returning to the site. This storage supports account access, session renewal, and sign-out.

Storage Names and Duration

Supabase session storage typically uses a project-specific key beginning with sb- and ending with -auth-token. Local storage has no automatic browser expiry and can remain until sign-out or removal. Access tokens expire according to the authentication service settings, and refresh tokens allow the client to renew valid sessions. Older Guardian AI storage entries named guardian_access_token and guardian_user are removed by the current sign-in and sign-out flow.

Analytics and Advertising

The current website does not include first-party analytics or advertising tracking scripts, and the application does not explicitly set cookies through its own JavaScript. Essential authentication information is kept in local storage. This policy will be updated if optional tracking technologies are introduced.

Third-Party Services

Sign-in and registration use Cloudflare Turnstile to help distinguish people from automated traffic. The browser contacts Cloudflare for this security check, and the server verifies its result. The website also loads authentication software from Supabase, scripts from jsDelivr and Tailwind CSS, and fonts from Google Fonts. Requests to these services share connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, with those providers. Their own policies govern their services and any storage they use. Embedded or linked third-party services may have separate cookie practices.

Managing Browser Storage

You can review, block, or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. To remove the active session on this device, use Sign out and clear the site’s local storage if needed. Blocking or clearing essential storage may sign you out or prevent your session from being retained. Clearing site data does not delete your Guardian AI account.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when the website’s use of cookies or browser storage changes. Review this page for the current description of these technologies.
